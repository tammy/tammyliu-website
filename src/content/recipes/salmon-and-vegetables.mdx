---
title: Salmon & vegetables
duration: 20 mins
date: 2026-05-23
---

A weeknight staple. One pan, minimal cleanup.

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, chopped
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Lemon, for serving

## Steps

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Toss vegetables in 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on pan.
3. Nestle salmon fillets among the vegetables. Drizzle with remaining oil, season with garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
4. Roast 15–18 minutes until salmon flakes easily.
5. Squeeze lemon over everything and serve immediately.
